Safety. Only switch breakers on and off; never open the panel cover. If a plug or outlet feels hot, smells burnt, or looks discolored, stop using it and tell your landlord or an electrician. WhichPlug uses the U.S. 120 V numbers and the NEC rule that a continuous load counts at 125%. It does not replace an electrician.

Map one breaker

  1. Plug a lamp or phone charger into the outlets you want to test.
  2. At the panel, switch off one breaker.
  3. Tick every outlet that lost power, then switch the breaker back on.
